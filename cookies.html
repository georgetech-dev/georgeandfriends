Cookies & storage

Cookies and similar technologies remember information on your device. You decide which optional features to allow.

What is used?

The site remembers privacy choices and accessibility options using browser local storage. Our hosting and security services may use essential cookies. Google Maps, Facebook Page embeds and live chat connect to other services when you enable them. No advertising or analytics script has been added to this website.

Change your choices

Rejecting optional features leaves booking, manual address entry and the contact form available. The opening can still be replayed from the footer. Turning a category off stops future loading; services already loaded are removed by reloading the page. We remove this site’s chat identifiers when chat is turned off; we cannot delete cookies on another provider’s domain.

You can also clear storage in your browser settings. Removing device identifiers does not delete booking or chat records held by the business; contact us to request that.
